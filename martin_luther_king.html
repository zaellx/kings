Who he wasEARLY LIFE AND CALLING

If Martin Luther King was born into the tradition he'd later lead
He was born on January 15, 1929, in Atlanta, Georgia, and came from a comfortable middle-class family steeped in the tradition of the Southern Black ministry.
If Martin Luther King was an exceptional student
He was an exceptionally gifted student who entered Morehouse College at age 15, graduated valedictorian from Crozer Theological Seminary, and earned a Ph.D. in systematic theology from Boston University by age 26.

What he fought forTHE MOVEMENT

If Martin Luther King led a movement
He was an American civil rights activist and Baptist minister who also led the civil rights movement, from 1955 until his death in 1968.
If Martin Luther King chose his methods deliberately
He fought for racial equality, civil rights, and the end of legal segregation for African Americans by promoting nonviolent tactics — following the example of the man who influenced him, Mahatma Gandhi.
If Martin Luther King was honored by the world
In 1964, he became the youngest person at the time to receive the Nobel Peace Prize.

If he were here nowA CLASS REFLECTION

If Martin Luther King were starting his career today
"I would use modern communication technology to reach more people. I would take action through social media and peaceful protest, and I would work with young people to fight for equality."
If Martin Luther King had lived past 1968
He would have continued leading the Poor People's Campaign.
If Martin Luther King had not advocated for nonviolent resistance
The civil rights movement could have taken a much more violent turn across the United States.
